Can I use this AI tool for this?

For staff at Summer Foundation and Housing Hub. Tell me which organisation you’re working for, what you’re working with, and the tool you have in mind — I’ll tell you if it’s safe and which conditions apply.

For: Summer Foundation & Housing Hub staff Aligned to: Privacy Act 1988, OAIC AI guidance, NDIS Code of Conduct, NDIS Practice Standards, NDIA data sovereignty Version: Draft 2.1

Eight short questions, a clear verdict, and a specific answer about your tool, your data, and the conditions that apply.

Most of the data we touch involves real people’s lives — NDIS participants, housing seekers, OTs, research and co-design contributors. Some of it sits at the strictest end of the Privacy Act, and some categories are also covered by the NDIS Code of Conduct and (for Housing Hub) the NDIS Practice Standards. That doesn’t make AI off-limits, but it does mean we have to be specific about who you are, which tool, for which purpose, with which protections in place.

AI tools in scope

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot (Enterprise) — including Copilot Notetaker in Teams meetings — in our tenant
  • ChatGPT Enterprise — in our workspace
  • Claude for Work / Claude Enterprise — in our workspace
  • Gemini for Workspace (Enterprise) — in our workspace

Frameworks this guide reflects

  • Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles — APP 6 (use), APP 8 (cross-border), APP 11 (security)
  • OAIC Guidance on privacy and the use of commercially available AI products (October 2024)
  • NDIS Code of Conduct — applies to all NDIS providers, registered or unregistered; Principle 2 requires respect for the privacy of people with disability
  • NDIS Practice Standards (Core Module — Information Management) — applies to Housing Hub as a registered NDIS provider
  • NDIA data sovereignty requirement that NDIS-identifiable data is stored in Australia (including re-identifiable data, backups, archives, copies)
  • NDIS Commission AI position statements (Feb 2026), including on AI use in behaviour support plans
A note on the contradiction some of you have spotted. Staff have been told two things that sound contradictory: that Copilot Notetaker is okay for meetings with NDIS participants, and that sensitive information cannot be used with any AI tool. Both are partly true. This guide resolves the contradiction by introducing the concept of approved exceptions: tightly-defined use cases on properly-configured tools, sanctioned by the AI Working Group. Copilot Notetaker for meetings with NDIS participants is the first such exception, and applies to both Summer Foundation and Housing Hub.

It is a decision aid, not a substitute for our data-classification document or for the AI Working Group’s advice in edge cases. When in doubt, send a quick note to the AI Working Group rather than guessing.
